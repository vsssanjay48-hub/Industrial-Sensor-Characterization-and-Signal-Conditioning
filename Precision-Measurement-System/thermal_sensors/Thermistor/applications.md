# Thermistor Applications

## 1. Medical Devices
Clinical thermometers, tympanic thermometers, catheter tips. Range 35–42°C — peak sensitivity zone. 0.5 mm glass bead thermistors achieve τ < 100 ms. Calibrated individually. Resolution 0.01°C.

## 2. Battery Management Systems (BMS)
10 kΩ NTC embedded in Li-ion packs. Standard B = 3435 K or 3950 K (BMS ICs have built-in tables). Monitors charge/discharge temperature limits (0–45°C charge, up to 60°C discharge). Prevents thermal runaway.

## 3. HVAC Thermostats
Room temperature sensing at −10 to +50°C. High sensitivity (< 0.1°C resolution) for comfortable deadband control. Low cost vs RTD. Factory calibrated.

## 4. Cold Chain Monitoring
Pharmaceutical cold storage (2–8°C), vaccine transport (2–8°C), ultra-low freezers (−80°C). Battery-powered loggers require microamp excitation — thermistors ideal. Continuous 15-minute logging.

## 5. Automotive
Engine coolant temperature (ECT): −40 to +135°C, sealed brass housing. Intake air temperature (IAT). Cabin HVAC control. ECU reads thermistor as voltage divider.

## 6. Consumer Electronics
Smartphone and laptop thermal management. USB-C charger overtemperature protection. CPU thermal throttling. Embedded in PCB near heat sources.

## Application Selection Guide
| Application | Range | Key Need | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical thermometer | 35–42°C | ±0.1°C, fast | 10kΩ glass bead |
| BMS | −20 to 60°C | Low cost, standard | 10kΩ SMD |
| HVAC | −10 to 50°C | ±0.5°C | 10kΩ through-hole |
| Cold chain | −40 to 10°C | Low power | 10kΩ, low-T char. |
| Automotive coolant | −40 to 135°C | Rugged | Sealed brass housing |
