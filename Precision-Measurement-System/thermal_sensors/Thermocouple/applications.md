# Thermocouple Applications

## 1. Industrial Furnaces and Kilns
Ceramic kilns: 1000–1300°C (Type K in alumina tube). Glass furnaces: 1300–1600°C (Type S or B). Heat treatment furnaces: 600–1100°C (Type K, zone control with multiple sensors).

## 2. Power Generation
Gas turbines: Exhaust gas temperature (EGT) at 800–1200°C (Type K or N). Safety-critical — exceeding EGT limits destroys engines. Steam boilers: 400–600°C (Type K or J).

## 3. Aerospace
Jet engine EGT shown in cockpit directly from Type K thermocouples. Re-entry vehicle heat shield temperature monitoring. Wind tunnel temperature profile mapping.

## 4. Materials Processing
Molten steel or aluminum: disposable Type K dipped in for single-use measurement (1400–1700°C). Plastic extrusion barrel: 150–300°C (Type J or K flush-mount). Semiconductor CVD reactors: 600–1100°C in quartz tubes.

## 5. Food Industry (Type T)
Type T (Copper/Constantan): best accuracy at low temperatures. Range −200 to +350°C. Food-safe copper. Used for freezer monitoring, pasteurization, cooking validation (HACCP critical control points).

## Selection Guide
| Application | Temperature | Type | Sheath |
|---|---|---|---|
| General industrial | 0–1000°C | K | SS 316 |
| Cryogenics / food | −200 to 350°C | T | Copper or SS |
| Molten metal | 1300–1600°C | S or B | Alumina |
| High-accuracy lab | 0–1400°C | S | Alumina |
| Cost-sensitive < 750°C | 0–750°C | J | SS 304 |
| Stable high-temp | 0–1300°C | N | SS or Inconel |
